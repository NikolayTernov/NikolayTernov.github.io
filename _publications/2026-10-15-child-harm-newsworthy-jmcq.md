Using more than 230,000 Telegram news posts from Kazakhstan (2020–2025) and 1,571 identified incidents of child harm, the study tests news-values and ideal-victim explanations of media attention with negative binomial regression. Event characteristics predict attention more strongly than victim characteristics: fatal interpersonal violence receives more coverage, while systemic failures receive less.
